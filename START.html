About this review set

Standalone pages present the design and support navigation, language switching and search filtering. The interactive prototype retains the original simulated workflows and requires internet access for its framework. The supplied color palette is retained, with Roboto for English and Noto Kufi Arabic for Arabic as requested. Both font families and the rounded icons are included for offline viewing.
